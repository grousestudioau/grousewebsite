Starting at the gym is a common goal, and a lot of us wait for the “perfect” moment to begin. The new year, the end of a busy stretch, once life settles down. The best time to start is now, and the trick is fitting training into the life you’ve already got so it sticks.

1. Put your health first

Your health isn’t up for negotiation. Like eating and sleeping, training should be a normal part of your week. Think of it as money in the bank for later. Without the body, you’ve got nothing.

2. Start small

You don’t need hours at the gym. Start with something you can manage, like 10 minutes, and build from there as it gets easier. Small steps stack up over time. Every rep counts.

3. Schedule your workouts

Treat your sessions like any other appointment. Put them in the calendar and stick to them. Consistency does the heavy lifting, and a set time makes it far more likely you’ll follow through.

4. Stay flexible

Life doesn’t run to plan, and a rigid schedule can trip you up. If you miss the morning, find a slot at lunch or in the evening. The aim is to move most days, not to be perfect about when.

5. Keep it convenient

Pick a gym close to home or work. The closer it is, the less it feels like a mission to get there, and the more often you’ll go.

6. Make it social

Training with friends or in a group class makes it more enjoyable and keeps you honest. We’re social animals, so doing it alongside other people tends to feel better.

7. Mix it up

Keep the routine varied so it stays interesting. Try HIIT one day, strength another, and something different to finish the week. View the Grouse Schedule.

8. Keep the payoff in mind

Better mood, more energy, better sleep, better health overall. On the busy days, that’s the stuff that gets you out the door.

9. Get some guidance

If you’re new or not sure where to start, think about personal training. A coach can tailor things to you and get you moving in the right direction faster.

10. Mark the progress

Notice the wins, however small. Every step forward counts, and it’s worth acknowledging.

Your health isn’t waiting around for a spare moment. So why should you? The best time to start is now.
