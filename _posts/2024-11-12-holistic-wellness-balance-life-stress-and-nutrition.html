At Grouse we reckon real health goes well beyond the walls of the gym. It’s about a life that looks after your body and your head at the same time, and that’s what keeps you healthy for the long haul. A few of the parts that matter sit outside your training, and these are the ones worth getting right.

Balancing life

Life gets busy and balance is hard to find. A lot of people end up under-recovered, with the demands of the day outrunning their rest. Work, family, friends and your own time all have to fit somewhere, so it helps to set clear priorities and a few boundaries. Block out time for the things that recharge you, whether that’s training, a hobby, or doing nothing at all. Saying no to something now and then is part of protecting your health.

Managing stress

Stress is a big one. It hits everyone differently, and sometimes what feels like another problem is stress wearing a disguise. Left unchecked over a long stretch it takes a real toll on your body and mind. Build in things that bring it down, like a walk outside, some slow breathing, or a bit of quiet. Training helps here too, which is part of why moving with a group does more than the physical side on its own.

Food

Good food is the base of all of it. Feed yourself mostly whole, unprocessed stuff, and aim for a mix of fruit, veg, decent protein and healthy fats. Eat with a bit of attention, notice when you’re hungry and when you’ve had enough, and give yourself time to taste it. Planning a few meals ahead makes it much easier on the busy days. Sleep and recovery

Sleep is where recovery happens, so aim for 7 to 9 solid hours. Your body likes predictability, and when your sleep is all over the place it can’t settle into a proper recovery routine. Go to bed and get up at around the same time each day, and give yourself a wind-down that tells your body it’s nearly time, like reading or a warm bath.

How you feel

How you feel matters as much as the rest. Spend time with people who lift you, and do things that leave you better than they found you. Our classes are part of that for a lot of members, both the training and the company. Even a short walk clears the head. And if you’re struggling with your mental health, reach out to a professional.
