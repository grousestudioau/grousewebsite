When people think about joining a gym, they think about the physical side. Getting stronger, dropping a bit of weight, sorting out their fitness. The part that tends to matter just as much is the people you end up doing it around.

Training on your own takes a lot of self-discipline, and most of us run low on it eventually. Training alongside people who show up to the same class is a different thing. You work a bit harder with others in the room, you drift less, and on the mornings you’d rather stay in bed, knowing someone will notice you’re missing is often enough to get you there.

You get to know your neighbours

Most people who train at Grouse live nearby. In a normal week you don’t get many chances to meet the people around you. A class gives you a relaxed way to do it, and those hellos often carry on outside the gym. It makes the area feel a bit smaller, in a good way.

A bit of friendly competition helps

Watching someone next to you hit a new best has a way of lifting your own effort. It doesn’t need to be serious. Even a shared class or a group challenge makes the work more interesting, and you push a little harder without having to talk yourself into it.

There’s always a coach on hand

Being in a room with our coaches means you’re never left guessing. They’ll answer questions, fix your form, and keep you working safely. You get the benefit of the knowledge without having to work it all out on your own.

It’s easier to stick with

This is the real one. When you feel like you belong somewhere, you keep turning up, and turning up over months and years is what gets results. When a session doubles as seeing people you like, it stops being another job on the list.

So joining a gym isn’t only about the workout. Come in, meet a few people, and do the work alongside them. It holds together a lot better that way.
