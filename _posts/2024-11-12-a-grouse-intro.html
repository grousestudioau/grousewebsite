Grouse is here to help you balance your health.

Exercise matters, but it can crowd out everything else. What about your food, your sleep, your work, your relationships and the rest of your life?

Our coaches are in it too, and not just the workouts. Most of us have full-time jobs and responsibilities on top of coaching, so the guidance you get comes from people living the same balancing act.

It’s easier again when you’re doing it with people you know. Most who come along are from right around the corner, so you’ve probably passed them on the street or seen them at your local cafe. The main draw is the smaller group classes, and they’re always tailored to what you need that day.

At Grouse, we keep it simple.
