Not sure if Grouse is right for you? Ask anything.

Quick answers.

Do I need to book before coming?

Yes. All sessions are booked through the app. You can sign up or log in here.

Can beginners join?

Yes. Every session is coached and every exercise scales to your level. You don’t need any experience. Just show up and let the coach know it’s your first time.

How does the first-session offer work?

Your first session is 50% off ($17.50). Join within your first 14 days and what you’ve spent on sessions comes off your first payment. Book it here.

What should I bring?

Training shoes, water, a sweat towel, and yourself. Everything else is here.

Is parking available?

Yes. There’s street parking on Victoria Road and surrounding streets. Early morning sessions are easy to park for.

Can I rent the studio for personal training or events?

Yes. See the rental page for details and availability.

Rather just come in?

Your first session is half price. Book a time and introduce yourself.
