The program

The Strength Loop.

Four qualities, cycled year-round. Each has its own focus and builds on the last, so you keep progressing without starting over.

Monday

Strength: Load

Compound lifts at challenging weights, with full focus on form.

Tuesday

Strength: Power

Explosive, lighter-weight work for speed and timing. The kind of power that’s easiest to neglect.

Wednesday

Strength: Foundation

The day that fixes the small things, so the heavy sessions feel better.

Thursday

Strength: Volume

Higher-rep, repeatable sets that build the base everything else sits on.
