Common questions.

Is there a contract?

No. Memberships run week to week, paid in advance. Cancel any time by contacting Josh.

Do unused sessions roll over on 2× a Week?

No, sessions reset each week. If you’re training more than twice a week regularly, Unlimited works out better.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Contact Josh and he’ll sort it out.

What’s the referral deal?

Bring your person. When they join, your next week’s on us, credited straight to your membership.

How do I book sessions?

Through the Mindbody app. You can sign up or log in from the Login link in the menu.

Are beginners welcome?

Always. Every session is coached and every exercise scales to your level. You don’t need any experience to start.
