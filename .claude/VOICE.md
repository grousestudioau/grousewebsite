# Grouse — Writing Voice

The reference for anything written as Grouse: web copy, emails, Mindbody descriptions, blog, course material, forms. Consolidated 2026-08-02 from Josh's corrections across the year. When something here conflicts with an older doc, this wins.

**Voice canon (read these to hear it):** indieformer.com (home + /how-we-make-a-game-popular/) · the New Dad's Manifesto post (tone and structure, not its devices) · the current grousestudio.au homepage (Josh wrote it).

## Tone

Straight — say what you mean. Confident, not loud. Warm, not soft: respecting people sometimes means telling them what they don't want to hear. Aussie, less hype, more work. Don't perform the way American fitness brands do. Honest over impressive, every time the two compete.

## The tests

Everything published passes all three:
1. **True** — could we back it up to a member's face?
2. **Thought about** — or is it the default version anyone would write?
3. **Spoken** — would Josh say this sentence out loud? If it's copy you'd only ever write, cut it.

And the repair rule when something fails: **the fix is deletion, not substitution.** Never swap a flagged device for a fresh device. Say the point plainly or cut it.

## Banned constructions (each caught in real drafts)

- "Not X, it's Y" / corrective negation / antithesis in all forms
- "No X. No Y. Just Z." and any negative parallelism or negative anaphora
- Contrasting pairs as rhythm ("Motivation gets the posts. Discipline gets the results.")
- Rule of three; parallel sentence structures inside a paragraph
- Staccato fragment chains ("Slept. Fed. Switched on.")
- Summary beats, landing sentences, setup/payoff constructions, paragraph pinning
- Throat-clearing openers; performed enthusiasm
- Stacked noun phrases; nominalisation; corporate verbs (leverage, underscore, reflect)
- Hedging qualifiers
- Writerly aphorisms — if a line lands too clean and quotable, loosen it toward speech
- Personification of objects and abstractions. Watch-words: carries, holds, arrives, points at, asks of you, demands. Write who actually does what.
- Soft-interiority adverbs (quietly, gently, simply as emotional shading)
- Overly visual language that doesn't earn its place ("worth sitting with", "keep coming back to", "the whiteboard hears"). Functional documents get instructional register — a form is not a story.
- Metonyms leaned on as a device ("the room" over and over)
- A metaphor stretched past one use
- Vary sentence length unpredictably; don't settle into any rhythm

**Em dashes:** none at all. Not as a rhythm crutch, not the "one Josh would naturally put in". Zero per page. Rewrite so the sentence doesn't need one: use a full stop, a comma, or two sentences.

**Intensifiers:** filler intensifiers are banned (genuinely, really, truly, actually). His sentences breathe; AI sentences compress. Example, his own edit: "Nobody ever taught the why" (too clean) became "No one talks or teaches the why."

## Never invent

Commitments that weren't stated: response times, spot-holding, call lengths, guarantees, deadlines, offers. If Josh didn't say it, it doesn't go in — flag the gap instead.

## Words

**Lean on:** strong, strength, work, build, move, train, coach, show up, stick at it, do it well, honest, out there.

**Never:** unleash, transform, soar, crush, smash, beast, warrior, sculpt, shred, best self, limitless, elevate, empower, journey, premier, boutique, bespoke, game-changing, next-level, optimise, holistic.

**No grading or comparison words** on public surfaces — nothing that ranks Grouse against other gyms ("real coaching" implies others fake it) and nothing that ranks people against each other. Comparing someone to their own past self is fine ("better at 45 than you were at 35").

## Positioning rules (from the 2026 passes)

- **Aspiration before critique** in anything student- or member-facing. Open with who they want to be, then what's borrowed. The marketing critique is taught as a skill (the claim filter), never delivered as a scolding. "You have been groomed" and profit-blame lines are internal/coach-side only.
- Don't knock other methods. Sell what we do and the results.
- Sell direction for Summit: "This is for people who are about their health."
- The goal lives outside the gym. Destinations aren't aesthetic.

## Product-language rules

- **"Earn"** is banned around food (diet-culture flashpoint). Carbs publicly = a performance tool, match them to the work; "non-essential" is the internal science framing, never the public lead.
- **"Crash-course"** banned — crash is diet culture's word and it prices a $3,200 course at $49.
- Every class is coached — no copy may imply otherwise (Unlimited + Coaching adds a coach who runs YOUR program).
- Prices display GST-inclusive, never "+GST".
